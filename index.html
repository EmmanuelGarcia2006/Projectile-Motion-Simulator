About the Project

This project explores projectile motion using MATLAB. The purpose is to compare the trajectory of a projectile with and without air resistance and understand how different physical variables influence its movement.

The simulation allows users to change the initial velocity, launch angle, projectile mass, and surface area.

Physics and Mathematical Models

Without Air Resistance

Horizontal position:

x(t) = v₀ cos(θ)t

Vertical position:

y(t) = v₀ sin(θ)t - ½gt²

With Air Resistance

Drag force:

Fᴅ = ½ρCᴅAv²

Air resistance acts opposite to the direction of motion and changes the projectile's acceleration and trajectory.

MATLAB Simulator

The MATLAB application accepts four user inputs: initial velocity, launch angle, mass, and surface area. It calculates projectile motion under both conditions and generates comparison graphs.

Conclusion

This project demonstrates how MATLAB can be used to model projectile motion and analyze the effects of air resistance. Comparing both models helps demonstrate the importance of drag forces in real-world projectile motion.
